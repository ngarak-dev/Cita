# Sharer interviews (10–20)

Goal: learn why people share Cita cards and what would make them share weekly.  
Time: 15–20 min. Recruit from Instagram/TikTok commenters, Discord, friends who already shared.

## Screener

- Shared ≥1 anime quote card (any app) in the last month?  
- Uses Instagram Stories / TikTok / WhatsApp Status?

## Script

1. Walk me through the last time you shared a quote or aesthetic post.  
2. What made that line worth posting?  
3. Open Cita (or show a Cita card). What’s clear / confusing in 10 seconds?  
4. Would you post this to Stories as-is? Why / why not?  
5. Watermark — ignore, hate, or fine if elegant?  
6. Daily Drop / widget — would you use a morning reminder?  
7. Pay once to remove ads while making cards?  
8. Pay monthly for no watermark + exclusive templates?  
9. What’s missing vs CapCut / Canva / screenshot + font app?  
10. Who would you invite, and with what message?
