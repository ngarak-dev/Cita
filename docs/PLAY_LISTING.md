## Positioning

Cita is an **anime quote studio** — craft shareable cards from the lines that define you.  
Not a commodity “600+ anime database.”

**Tagline:** Make the lines that made you.

## Title (≤30 characters)

## Full description

```
Cita turns iconic anime lines into shareable cards.

Make the lines that made you — pick a quote, style it in Card Studio, and share to Stories or Feed.

• Card Studio templates (Classic, Midnight, Resolve, Heartbreak, Chaos, Ember, Mist, Ink, Neon + Aurora unlock)
• Stories & Feed export formats
• Daily Drop + home-screen widget
• Mood lanes, favorites & personal collections
• Offline quotes from beloved series — curated depth over empty catalogs
• Invite friends to unlock Aurora

Built for fans who post what they feel — not another quote dump.

Search: anime quote maker, anime story quotes, anime quote cards, otaku quotes
```

## Screenshot / feature-graphic notes (when you ship)

1. **Hero:** Card Studio export (Stories) with a strong resolve line — brand “Cita” readable.  
2. **Daily Drop** home with unique abstract cover (not licensed key art).  
3. **Template row** showing 3–4 distinct styles.  
4. **Favorites / collections** (“My resolve”, “Cry lines”).  
5. **Anime grid** with lettermark covers (typography-first).

Avoid: fake “600+ anime” claims, scraped character posters, cluttered UI chrome.
