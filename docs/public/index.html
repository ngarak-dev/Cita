1. About Cita

Cita (“quote” in Spanish) is a free Android app that lets you browse anime quotes offline and turn them into shareable cards. Quotes are bundled in the app for offline use. You can view quotes by anime, browse lists, get random quotes, save favorites, save a quote as an image on your device, and share it.

This page explains what information the app may process, how it is used, and the rules for using Cita. It is intended for Google Play and end users.

2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Information we do not collect directly

Cita does not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos from your camera, or microphone access. We do not sell your personal information.

2.2 Information processed automatically

Depending on your device and settings, the following may be processed:

  • App analytics & diagnostics — via Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (for example crash reports, basic device/app usage metrics) to keep the app stable and improve quality.
  • Advertising identifiers & ad interaction data — via Google AdMob, which may use advertising IDs and similar signals subject to Google’s policies and your device privacy controls.
  • Network requests — when ads or analytics SDKs communicate with their servers. Quote browsing itself works offline from content bundled in the app.
  • Local preferences — small values stored on your device (for example SharedPreferences for favorites and save credits) so features work between sessions. This data stays on your device.

2.3 Quote images you save

When you choose to save a quote as an image, the image is written to storage on your device (for example an app folder such as “Cita”). Those files are not uploaded to our servers.

2.4 Sharing

If you use the system share sheet (for example WhatsApp, Gmail, or other apps), content you share is handled by the app you select under that app’s own privacy policy. Cita only passes the content you choose to share.

2.5 Third-party services

  • Google Privacy Policy (Firebase, AdMob, Play services)
  • AdMob & privacy

2.6 Children

Cita is a general-audience entertainment app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party SDKs in the app, contact us so we can help address it with the relevant providers where possible.

2.7 Data retention & your choices

Local app data can be cleared by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in Android settings. You can limit ad personalization and advertising ID use in your device’s Google / privacy settings. Crash and analytics retention follows Google Firebase / AdMob policies.

2.8 Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or legal requirements change. The “Effective date” at the top will be revised. Continued use of Cita after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

4. Permissions Explained

  • Internet / network state — used to load ads and analytics. Quote browsing works offline.
  • Storage (older Android versions) — used only when you save a quote image. On newer Android versions, saving uses app-scoped storage where possible and may not need a broad storage permission.

Cita does not use camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or location permissions.

5. Advertising

Cita is free and supported by Google AdMob (banners, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads). Rewarded ads may unlock saving more quotes without seeing another ad for a period, as described in the app. Ad partners may collect device and usage data as described in their policies. You can manage ad preferences in your Google account / device settings where available.
